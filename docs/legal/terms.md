## 3. Your household and its roles

Foon works by household. One household has one subscription and everyone in it shares the same plan, library and
lists. Everyone in a household can see what the others add (see the Privacy Policy).

- **Owner:** the parent who holds the subscription and the household's settings. Only the owner can delete the
  household. Handing ownership to another parent is planned but not available yet.
- **Parents (guardians):** adults who manage people: add and invite them, set roles, remove them, change allergies, and
  give or withdraw consent for allergies and for kids.
- **Planners:** build and change the week and manage the meal library. Planners can export the household's data.
- **Members:** request meals, vote, rate, add to lists and offer to cook.

The owner is responsible for the household: for who is invited, for keeping the household's information accurate, and
for the subscription. Parents are responsible for the consents they give. Removing someone ends their access. Deleting
the household erases it for everyone.

## 5. Allergy flags are a helper, not a guarantee

**Please read this carefully.**

**Foon's allergy flags are a helper, not a guarantee. They can be wrong or missing. Always read ingredient labels,
check with the people you're cooking for, and follow the advice of your doctor. Foon does not give medical advice.**

Foon flags a dish when its ingredients, as typed into Foon, match words we link to an allergen. That means:

- a dish with no ingredients, or ingredients typed differently, may not be flagged;
- packaged foods, brands, cross-contact and recipes from other sites are not checked;
- no flag doesn't mean a dish is safe.

You are responsible for what you cook and serve. Never rely on Foon alone for someone's allergy.
