This policy covers the one kind of health information Foon keeps: food allergies. Washington's My Health My Data Act
(RCW 19.373) and Nevada's consumer health data law (NRS 603A.400 to 603A.550) ask for it to be separate from our
[Privacy Policy](privacy.md). Foon is an app made by The Recruiting Compass LLC ("Foon", "we", "us").

> nothing here goes beyond that.

## What we collect, why, and how we use it

- **What.** For each person in a household: which of the 9 major US food allergens apply, and any other food
  allergies a parent types in.
- **Why.** To flag dishes and shopping items that contain them.
- **How we use it.** Only for that flagging. We don't use allergies for anything else. They're never used for ads,
  never sold, never sent anywhere for AI, and never put in a notification's text.

We keep allergies only after a parent agrees in the app (Agree and save). The server refuses to store an allergy
without that agreement.

> **Review:** a parent can type any text as an "other" allergy. Should this say that it could include something other
> than a food allergy? [CHECK]

## Where it comes from

A parent enters it in the app, for themselves or for someone in their household. We don't infer allergies from anything
else, and we don't get them from anyone else.

## Who we share it with

**Outside your household: nobody.** We don't share allergies with any third party or affiliate. We have no affiliates.

**Inside your household:** everyone in your household can see each person's allergies, so the whole family knows why
a dish is flagged. Only parents can add or change them; an adult can remove their own.

> **Review:** confirm that household members seeing each other's allergies isn't "sharing" with a third party under
> RCW 19.373.010(27). If it is, separate consent to share is needed.

Our hosting provider, Supabase, stores allergies for us as our processor, under a contract, and only to run Foon. That
isn't sharing under the act. [CHECK: processor contract on file.]

## Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to:

- **Confirm and see** whether we collect your health data, and get a copy. Everyone in the household sees allergies in
  the app (on each person's profile in Family). We share them with no third parties, so there's no third-party list
  to give.
- **Correct or remove your own.** An adult who isn't a parent can remove all of their own allergies on their profile
  in Family (**Remove my allergies**); a parent does it with **Change allergies**. Only parents can add or change
  allergies in the app, so an adult who isn't a parent can ask a parent in their household, or write to us, to
  correct theirs.
- **Withdraw consent.** An adult withdraws for themselves by removing their own allergies (above): they're deleted at
  once. A parent can turn **Allergy alerts** off in Family, which withdraws consent for the household and erases every
  allergy recorded for it at once.

> **Review:** after an adult removes their own, a parent can still add them again under the household's agreement.
> Chris decided not to block that (2026-11-07); counsel to confirm it's enough for withdrawal under RCW
> 19.373.040(1)(b). [CHECK]
- **Delete.**
  - A parent can remove anyone's allergies on their profile at any time, and an adult can remove their own.
  - Deleting an adult's account deletes their allergies. A kid's allergies stay on the profile their parent manages,
    and a parent can remove them.
  - Deleting the household deletes everyone's.
  - Deleted allergies leave our hosting provider's backups within 7 days.

If you can't do this in the app, or you'd rather ask us, write to support@getfoon.com. We'll check that the request
comes from you, or from a parent for a child.

- We'll answer within 45 days. If we need longer, we'll tell you why within those 45 days and take at most 45 more.
- An emailed deletion request is carried out within 30 days of confirming who you are.
- Requests are free, up to twice a year.
- You don't need to create an account to make one.

**Appeals.** If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer or writing to support@getfoon.com
with "Appeal" in the subject. We'll answer in writing within 45 days and explain what we decided and why. If we still
say no, you can contact your state's attorney general:

## No new uses without asking

We won't collect other kinds of health data, or use or share allergies for any other purpose, without first telling
you and getting your consent.
